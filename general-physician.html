GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

What should I bring?

Bring recent reports, a list of medicines and any previous records relevant to your concern. The patient guide explains how to prepare.

Can I request a follow-up?

Yes. Choose the relevant service in the appointment form and mention that you would like a follow-up. The clinic team will confirm availability.

Does a booking form confirm my appointment?

The form prepares a WhatsApp request. Your visit is confirmed when the clinic team replies.
