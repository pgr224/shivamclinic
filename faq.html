Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does the clinic treat?

We treat common acute illnesses, manage chronic conditions, and provide preventive care for all ages.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended to reduce waiting time; walk-ins are seen as availability permits.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring previous medical records, test reports, a list of current medicines and any known allergies.

Are children treated?

Yes. We provide paediatric consultations, growth monitoring and vaccination guidance.

How often should I have a health check-up?

Most healthy adults benefit from an annual check-up; your doctor may advise more frequent screening based on risk factors.

Is my medical information kept confidential?

Yes. Patient records are handled confidentially; see our Privacy Policy.

Can I get a follow-up consultation?

Follow-ups are encouraged for chronic conditions and after treatment to monitor progress.

What should I do in a medical emergency?

Contact your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital emergency department immediately.
