GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

How do I choose a treatment?

A consultation helps you compare suitable options. Your skin, health history, current products and previous treatments are part of that discussion.

Will I need more than one visit?

The plan depends on the concern and treatment being considered. Ask about the likely visit schedule and review points before proceeding.

Where can I learn about facials?

Our facials and skin-care page introduces HydraFacial, carbon facial and personalised skin care. You can also ask which type of consultation fits your concern.
