Skin Care in the Monsoon: Preventing Fungal Infections

Ideas and information for your everyday wellbeing.

Home/Journal
✳

Illustrative image · Health journal

Published 2026

Humidity encourages fungal and bacterial skin infections.

Prevention

Keep skin dry, wear loose cotton clothing, change damp clothes promptly and avoid sharing towels.

Treatment

Persistent itching or rashes need a proper diagnosis; avoid self-medicating with steroid creams.
