Diabetes Diet Basics: Eating Well to Control Blood Sugar

Published 2026

Food choices strongly influence blood sugar. Focus on balance, portion size and consistency.

Choose

Whole grains, legumes, vegetables, nuts and lean proteins.

Limit

Sugary drinks, refined flour, sweets and heavily processed foods.

Habits

Eat at regular times, stay active for about 150 minutes a week, and monitor glucose as your doctor advises.
