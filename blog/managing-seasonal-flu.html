Managing Seasonal Flu: Symptoms, Care and Prevention

Published 2026

Seasonal influenza causes fever, body aches, sore throat, cough and fatigue. Most healthy people recover within a week with rest and fluids.

Home care

Rest, drink plenty of fluids, and use fever reducers as advised by your doctor. Avoid antibiotics unless a bacterial infection is confirmed.

Prevention

Annual vaccination, regular hand washing and avoiding close contact with sick people lower your risk.

When to see a doctor

Seek care for breathing difficulty, persistent high fever, chest pain, or if you are pregnant, elderly or have a chronic illness.
