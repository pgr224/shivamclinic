Child Vaccination Guide: Why Timely Immunisation Matters

Published 2026

Vaccines protect children from serious diseases such as measles, polio, diphtheria and hepatitis B.

Staying on schedule

Follow the national immunisation schedule and keep a vaccination card. Ask your doctor about optional vaccines.

Side effects

Mild fever or soreness is common and usually resolves in a day or two.
