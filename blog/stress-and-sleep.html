Stress and Sleep: Simple Habits for Better Health

Published 2026

Poor sleep and chronic stress feed each other and can affect blood pressure, mood and immunity.

Tips

Keep a fixed sleep time, limit screens and caffeine at night, exercise regularly and practise breathing or relaxation techniques.

Seek help

If sleeplessness lasts over a few weeks, consult a doctor.
