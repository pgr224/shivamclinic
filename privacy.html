How this website and the appointment request work.

Your appointment request

The appointment form prepares an editable message on your device and opens WhatsApp. This website does not submit the form to a clinic server or save its fields in browser storage. The clinic receives the details only if you send the message in WhatsApp.

Please avoid including sensitive medical information in this request. Once you choose to use WhatsApp, its own terms and privacy policy apply. The clinic will confirm appointment availability directly.

Third-party services

Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the contact page embeds a Google Maps view. Your browser contacts those services to display them. WhatsApp and map direction links open third-party services when selected. Treatment images are hosted with this website.

Questions about your information

For questions about information shared with the clinic, please contact the clinic team. Medical record handling is separate from this static website.
