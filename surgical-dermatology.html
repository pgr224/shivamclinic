GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

Can I choose a procedure before the visit?

You can ask about a procedure when requesting a consultation. An assessment is needed before a treatment decision.

What will the consultation cover?

The discussion can include your concern, relevant health history, suitable options, possible risks and follow-up. Bring records of earlier treatment where available.

Will a procedure happen at my first visit?

An appointment request is for a consultation. Any procedure, preparation and timing are discussed separately after assessment.
