GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

Which facial should I book?

You can select “Specialty facials & skin care” and ask the clinic team for guidance. You do not need to choose a procedure before your consultation.

What should I tell the clinician?

Mention skin sensitivity, allergies, current products and recent treatments. Bring relevant prescriptions or reports if you have them.

What about aftercare?

Ask for instructions for your chosen treatment, including what to use, what to avoid and when to arrange a review. Guidance depends on the treatment and your skin.
