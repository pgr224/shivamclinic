GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

Is my appointment confirmed when WhatsApp opens?

No. Review and send the draft, then wait for the clinic team to reply with availability. The website does not confirm a time slot.

Do I need to share medical details in the form?

Keep the request brief. Avoid including sensitive medical information in the optional message; discuss your health history during the consultation.

What if I do not know which doctor to choose?

Leave the doctor preference as “Please advise me” and select the service closest to your concern. The clinic team can help you arrange an appropriate consultation.

How do I find the clinic?

The contact page has the clinic’s owner-provided address, opening hours, map and directions. Call or message the team if you need help planning your visit.
