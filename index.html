before you visit.

More common questions ↗
Where should I start if I’m not sure which service I need?

Choose “Not sure — please advise” on the appointment form, or call our team. We’ll help you find a starting point.

How do I book a visit?

Complete the appointment form to open an editable WhatsApp request. Send it to the clinic and wait for our team to confirm availability.

Can I discuss a treatment before deciding?

Yes. A consultation is a chance to discuss your concerns, suitability, expected costs, aftercare and alternatives before making a decision.
